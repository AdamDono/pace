# Pace Tech — Comprehensive Lean Business Canvas
## FNB App of the Year (AOTY) Hackathon 2026

---

### 1. PROBLEM STATEMENT (BACKED BY EVIDENCE)
South Africa faces a severe talent and productivity crisis across the entire educational-to-employment pipeline. Over 60% of youth (ages 15–34) are unemployed (Stats SA), while over 700,000 Grade 10–12 learners navigate high school without career guidance, resulting in less than 38% achieving a university Bachelor's pass. Meanwhile, private tutoring costs R250–R500/hour, pricing out the majority of families when students begin failing critical STEM subjects.

At the workforce level, the gap widens: millions of unemployed youth and unskilled/semi-skilled workers are locked out of career mobility because university degrees and private coding/business bootcamps cost R40,000–R80,000 per year. Simultaneously, companies and skills development programs struggle with exorbitant corporate training costs to upskill junior staff, lacking accessible digital platforms to deliver custom-tailored, accredited vocational training that drives real workplace productivity.

---

### 2. SOLUTION & FEATURES
Pace Tech is an end-to-end academic intelligence and workforce upskilling ecosystem designed to grow learners from high school into high-value employment:
• High School Academic & APS Engine: Multimodal report card scanner extracts marks, computes official university APS scores, highlights career entry gaps, and builds adaptive weekly study timetables for struggle subjects.
• 24/7 Socratic AI Tutor: Voice and text learning companion providing real-time, step-by-step conceptual mastery across school STEM and professional technical subjects.
• Workforce Upskilling & Tailored Training Tracks: Self-paced, affordable courses in in-demand tech (coding, web development, data) and practical business skills with verifiable digital certificates.
• B2B Corporate Upskilling Portal: Custom training tracks for companies to train junior/unskilled staff, monitor employee progress, and generate compliance-ready reporting for B-BBEE skills development.
• Direct Accreditation Pathway: Actively aligning curriculums to secure in-house QCTO/SETA accreditation, slashing the cost of recognized vocational qualifications by up to 80%.
