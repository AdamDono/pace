Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit an assignment?
Navigate to the course, click on the assignment section, and click "Submit Assignment". You can upload files or write your response directly.
How do I download my certificate?
Complete all course sections and rate the course. Then go to Certificates in the sidebar and click Download.
Can I retake a quiz?
Yes! You can retake quizzes up to 3 times. Your highest score will be recorded.
How do I track my progress?
Click "My Progress" in the sidebar to see detailed progress for all your courses, including completion percentages and time spent.
