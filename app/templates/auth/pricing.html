Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Anti-Tamper Report Card Scanner and APS Calculator work? +

Learners upload a photo or PDF of their official school term report card. Our intelligent OCR engine automatically extracts each subject, grade level, and mark percentage, and locks them to prevent tampering. It then calculates your official university Admission Point Score (APS) based on South African university criteria (e.g., excluding Life Orientation where required) so you know exactly where you qualify.

Can I really speak to Bookworm AI using my voice? Does it know my syllabus? +

Yes! Bookworm features real-time voice conversation powered by natural neural speech synthesis. Simply tap the microphone icon to speak your question and listen to clear, step-by-step explanations. Even better, Bookworm is connected directly to your enrolled courses, syllabus chapters, upcoming assignment due dates, and recent report card marks—so it guides you on your actual schoolwork without generic responses.

What if I take Mathematical Literacy instead of Pure Mathematics? +

Our Career & Math Reality Gap Analyzer respects both tracks. You can type any career—from Software Engineer to Lawyer or Graphic Designer—and the system evaluates your math type. If a university degree strictly mandates Pure Mathematics (e.g., Engineering or Medicine), it alerts you honestly, highlights accessible TVET and National Diploma bridging paths, and recommends high-earning careers that thrive with Mathematical Literacy (e.g., Digital Marketing, HR Management, Business Operations, UX Design).

How does the Adaptive Weekly Study Timetable schedule my study time? +

Once your term report card is scanned, the platform identifies which subjects you scored under 55% in. The algorithm auto-prioritizes those struggle subjects by allocating focused revision blocks throughout your week, complete with CAPS curriculum topics and NSC past paper drills so you make steady improvements before exams.

What is included in the Varsity & Skills Pro plan? +

Varsity & Skills Pro bridges the gap between high school and career readiness. It includes everything in Matric Core (all 5 CAPS subjects, report card scanner, timetable, 24/7 Voice AI), plus 1 full accredited digital skills course (e.g., End User Computing or Web Development), verifiable QR certificates for your CV, and university application roadmaps (Wits, UCT, UP, UJ, and TVET colleges).

Are the digital skills certificates verified and accredited? +

Yes. Every certificate issued by Pace includes a cryptographic QR code that prospective employers, recruitment agencies, or universities can scan directly to verify authenticity. Our accredited courses are aligned with SETA and QCTO unit standards with registered assessor and moderator Portfolio of Evidence (PoE) evaluations.

Can corporates and SDPs claim B-BBEE and SETA discretionary grant funding? +

Absolutely. Pace provides audit-ready reporting packs for corporate B-BBEE Skills Development Scorecard compliance (Element 300) and enables Skills Development Providers (SDPs) and NGOs to manage SETA-funded learnerships with full digital Portfolios of Evidence (PoE) and NLRD export files.

What payment methods do you accept in South Africa? +

We accept convenient South African payment options including Capitec Pay, Instant EFT (Ozow / SiD), Visa & Mastercard debit/credit cards, and formal 30-day corporate invoices for schools, SDPs, and enterprise partners.
