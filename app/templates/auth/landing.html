The Story

This is an access problem.
Not a talent problem.

We built Pace Academy because we watched what happens when people have the talent but not the certificate. A young person who is brilliant at coding, design, or accounting but cannot afford the private college course that gets them through the door.

“That is not a skills problem. That is a systems problem.”

Pace connects educators, accreditation standards, and job-readiness in one place, making recognized qualifications accessible to anyone with a phone and a data connection.
