Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about enrollment, SETA/QCTO accreditation, and institutional licensing.

How does enrollment work if learners cannot self-enroll? ↓
To maintain high quality and manage cohort seat limits, learners click "Apply to Enroll" on any public course page to submit their application. Our admissions queue reviews applicant details and automatically dispatches their secure login credentials via email once approved.
Are qualifications SETA and QCTO accredited? ↓
Yes! Pace Academy hosts both NQF-aligned accredited occupational qualifications (QCTO and SETA mapped unit standards) and practical non-accredited short skills courses designed for rapid job readiness.
Can students study on a smartphone or low-data mobile connection? ↓
Absolutely. The entire Pace LMS platform is optimized for mobile responsiveness and low data consumption. Students can read course modules, take assessment quizzes, and even run code compiled directly inside their phone's browser using our WebAssembly sandbox engine.
How does institutional licensing work for high schools, colleges & corporates? ↓
We provide flat annual licensing tiers for schools, training providers, and corporate learnership sponsors. This gives your entire institution access to white-labeled academies, teacher curriculum authoring tools, automated homework grading, and real-time student progress monitoring dashboards.
How do students receive their certificates upon completion? ↓
Once a student completes all required module sections, assignments, and assessment quizzes, the system automatically generates an official downloadable PDF Certificate complete with a unique verification ID for employer validation.
