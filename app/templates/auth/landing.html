Our Story

Built for talent.
Backed by purpose.

We built Pace Academy because we watched what happens when people have the talent but not the certificate. Pace connects educators, accreditation standards, and job-readiness in one place, making recognized qualifications accessible to anyone with a phone and a data connection.

Our mission is simple: to break down every barrier between ambition and achievement. Whether you are a matriculant searching for your first qualification, a professional upskilling for a promotion, or an institution looking to scale training — Pace Academy exists to make sure your potential is never held back by access, cost, or geography.
