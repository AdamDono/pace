# Pace Academy - Strategic Product Roadmap 🚀

This document tracks core capabilities, strategic milestones, and premium features engineered to bridge South African youth from high school academic excellence into high-value employment.

---

## 🧠 Phase 5: Intelligent AI Socratic Tutor & Career Pathway Engine
*Detailed Architecture Specification:* [`docs/feature_proposals/ai_study_tutor_spec.md`](docs/feature_proposals/ai_study_tutor_spec.md)

- **Socratic Diagnostic AI Tutor:** Embedded in-course study companion that diagnoses student misconceptions through multi-turn root-cause questioning rather than giving away direct quiz answers.
- **Grade 10–12 CAPS High School Curriculum Alignment:** Deep academic support for Life Sciences, Physical Sciences, and Mathematics with interactive 3D spatial models (DNA double helix, heart anatomy, cellular structures).
- **Predictive Mastery & Early-Warning Telemetry (Hackathon "Intelligence" Focus):** Tracks 3D model interaction time, quiz drop-offs, and code compilation retries to forecast dropout and exam failure risk before it happens.
- **Dynamic APS & Career Pathway Forecaster:** Evaluates high school term report cards, calculates real-time Admission Point Scores (APS), and generates dual pathways into university degrees or direct-to-employment technical micro-credentials.
- **AI Course Blueprint & Authoring Assistant:** Tools for educators to generate structured lessons, assessments, and quiz questions from syllabus topics.

---

## 🧊 Phase 6: Spatial 3D Learning & Advanced Media
- **Interactive 3D Hotspot Authoring (Complete):** Direct WebGL/Three.js spatial model embedding with annotated clickable pins and responsive iframe viewers.
- **Interactive Video Layers:** Timestamp-triggered checkpoint quizzes embedded in video lectures.
- **Audio Lesson Reader:** Text-to-speech accessibility engine for data-efficient learning on low-end mobile devices.

---

## 🎓 Phase 7: Student Retention, Gamification & Certification
- **Verifiable Digital Credentials:** Cryptographically verifiable certificates with QR codes, custom signatures, and direct LinkedIn sharing.
- **Gamified Mastery Ladders:** Skill badges, streak milestones, and peer leaderboards for course progress.
- **SETA / QCTO Compliance Audit Tooling:** Automated generation of regulatory attendance, assessment logs, and portfolio-of-evidence (PoE) exports.

---

## 👥 Phase 8: Enterprise Scale & Collaboration
- **Bulk School / Enterprise Enrollment:** CSV/Excel student ingestion with automated batch account provisioning and notification emails.
- **Course Q&A & Mentorship Boards:** Integrated peer discussion and instructor escalation channels.
- **Co-Teaching & Departmental Collaboration:** Granular multi-instructor permissions on shared course units.
