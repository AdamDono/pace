# Pace Academy - Future Roadmap 🚀

This document tracks premium features and improvements planned for the next phases of development.

## 🧠 Phase 5: AI & Automation
- **AI Course Blueprint**: Allow teachers to generate a full course structure (Lessons & Chapters) just by typing a topic.
- **AI Content Assistant**: Help teachers write lesson content or generate quiz questions from text.

## 🎓 Phase 6: Student Engagement & Retention
- **Premium Certificate Design**: Upgrade the automated certificate generator to include high-fidelity, premium PDF designs with custom watermarks, signatures, and verifiable QR codes.
- **Interactive Video Layers**: Trigger pop-up questions at specific timestamps in videos to ensure student focus.
- **Gamification**: Add badges or experience points for completing lessons and scoring high on quizzes.

## 👥 Phase 7: Scale & Collaboration
- **Bulk Student Enrollment**: Upload a CSV/Excel file of student emails to enroll them in one click.
- **Teacher/Student Discussion Boards**: A "Help" or "Discuss" section inside every lesson for Q&A.
- **Collaborative Teaching**: Allow multiple teachers to contribute to the same course.
